Privacy Policy

Star Battle Nova for Android · Effective 5 August 2026

Star Battle Nova collects nothing, stores nothing about you, and sends nothing anywhere. It has no internet permission at all, so it is not capable of transmitting data even in principle. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

What is collected

Nothing. The app does not collect, request or derive any personal or device information. It never asks you to sign in, and there is nothing to sign in to.

What the app stores, and where

Playing the game creates a small amount of data, kept in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • your settings — chosen game piece, difficulty, sound, haptics and assist preferences;
  • the board you are part-way through, so it is still there when you come back;
  • your statistics — games started and solved, solve times, hints used.

This never leaves the device. It is not transmitted, not backed up to us, and not visible to other apps. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also clear it at any time from Android's Settings → Apps → Star Battle Nova → Storage → Clear storage.

The one exception: Android Backup

Android itself may copy app data to your own Google Drive as part of its standard device-backup feature, so a reinstall can restore your board and statistics. This is a platform feature operated by Google under Google's privacy policy, and the developer of this app neither receives nor can access that copy. You can turn it off in Android's system backup settings.

Permissions

The app requests exactly one Android permission:

  • VIBRATE — for the haptic feedback when you place a piece, make an illegal move, or solve a board. It can be switched off in the app's own settings.

In particular the app does not request the internet permission, which is what makes the statements above structural rather than a promise.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the release notes.
